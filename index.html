Shortcuts

Tap a filled tile to change that letter. Swipe left on the grid to clear the row. Hold backspace to delete quickly.

Practice

The ∞ button opens practice: unlimited words with their own stats. Pick a list above the grid: English, Easy (four letters), Malay, Indonesian or Filipino. Only English counts for the daily streak.

Streaks

A new word arrives at midnight. Solve it each day to build a streak. Every seven daily games earns a streak freeze that covers one missed day.
